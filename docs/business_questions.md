# Business questions

1. What share of delivered orders arrive after the estimated date?
2. Which states have the worst delivery performance?
3. How long does each stage take: approval, carrier handover, delivery?
4. Which sellers cause the most late deliveries?
5. How does a late delivery affect review scores?
6. Which product categories earn the most revenue, and which carry high freight costs?
7. What is the repeat-customer rate?
8. How do payment methods and instalments differ by order value?

# KPI definitions
- On-time delivery rate: delivered orders where the delivered date is on or before the estimated date, divided by all delivered orders.
- Cycle time: days from purchase to customer delivery.
- Late days: max(0, delivered date minus estimated date).
